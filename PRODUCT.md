# PRODUCT: EU-see

## Product Purpose
A personal tribute site for Eulyn, designed to be a soft, creative, and thoughtful space that reflects her personality, hobbies, and favorite things.

## Users
- **Primary:** Eulyn (the recipient).
- **Secondary:** Friends and the creator.

## Brand & Tone
- **Keywords:** Soft, Creative, Cute, Genuine, Warm, Easy-to-appreciate.
- **Tone:** Appreciative, personal, and slightly whimsical. It should feel like a digital letter or a scrapbook, not a corporate landing page.
- **Anti-references:** Corporate SaaS, aggressive marketing, "modern minimalist" clinical look, neon/gaming aesthetics.

## Strategic Principles
- **Intimacy:** Focus on small details and personal touches.
- **Softness:** Use rounded corners, gentle gradients, and organic layouts.
- **Authenticity:** Let the photos and personal lists be the hero.
