# DESIGN: EU-see

## Visual Strategy
**Digital Scrapbook / Modern Letter**. The design aims to feel like a handmade physical object—specifically a linen journal or a pressed flower diary. It avoids corporate clinical looks in favor of warmth, texture, and organic imperfection.

## Typography
- **Headings:** `Young Serif`. A chunky, friendly, and personality-rich serif that feels "human" and crafted.
- **Body:** `Hanken Grotesk`. A humanistic sans-serif that provides clarity while maintaining a soft, approachable tone.
- **Eyebrows:** All-caps `Hanken Grotesk` with letter spacing for a "labeled" look.

## Color Palette (OKLCH)
- **Background:** `oklch(98.5% 0.01 85)` (Warm cream paper).
- **Ink:** `oklch(25% 0.02 260)` (Deep ink blue, soft on the eyes).
- **Accent (Blue Lily):** `oklch(65% 0.12 250)` (Vibrant but soft blue).
- **Warm Accent:** `oklch(92% 0.04 40)` (Blush/Peach for secondary cards).

## Elements & Texture
- **Texture:** A subtle SVG noise filter is applied to the background and as a fixed overlay to simulate paper grain.
- **Elevation:** Soft, multi-layered shadows using deep ink tints at low opacity.
- **Organic Movement:** Photos and hero cards are slightly rotated (`-2deg` to `2deg`) to mimic items placed by hand.
- **Transitions:** Staggered reveals and subtle lifts on hover.

## Layout
- **Hero:** Asymmetric layout with a dominant typographic headline and a "taped" feature card.
- **Collage:** A horizontal slider of "Polaroid-style" cards with white borders and varied tilts.
- **Grids:** Responsive `auto-fit` grids that breathe with generous padding.
